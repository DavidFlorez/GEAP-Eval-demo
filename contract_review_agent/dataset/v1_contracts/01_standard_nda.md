# MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AND CONFIDENTIALITY AGREEMENT

**Agreement Number:** NDA-2024-0881  
**Effective Date:** January 15, 2024  
**Dataset Version:** v1.0  

This Mutual Non-Disclosure and Confidentiality Agreement ("**Agreement**") is entered into by and between:

1. **Aegis Cloud Systems, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation having its principal place of business at 500 Technology Way, Wilmington, DE 19801 ("**Aegis**"); and
2. **Veritas BioHealth Labs, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company having its principal place of business at 1200 Innovation Parkway, Suite 400, Dover, DE 19904 ("**Veritas**").

Aegis and Veritas may collectively be referred to as the "**Parties**" or individually as a "**Party**." The Party disclosing information is the "**Disclosing Party**," and the Party receiving information is the "**Receiving Party**."

---

### RECITALS

WHEREAS, the Parties wish to explore potential strategic business collaborations, technical integrations, and prospective joint commercial transactions concerning secure distributed cloud infrastructure for healthcare diagnostics (the "**Permitted Purpose**"); and

WHEREAS, in connection with evaluating the Permitted Purpose, each Party may disclose to the other Party certain proprietary, sensitive, non-public technical, commercial, operational, and financial information;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants contained herein and other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows:

---

### SECTION 1: DEFINITION OF CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION

1.1. "**Confidential Information**" means any and all non-public information disclosed by the Disclosing Party to the Receiving Party, whether orally, electronically, visually, or in tangible written form, that is designated as "Confidential," "Proprietary," or with a similar legend, or that reasonably should be understood to be confidential given the nature of the information and the circumstances of disclosure.

1.2. Confidential Information includes, without limitation: source code, software architectures, algorithms, benchmark tests, product roadmaps, research data, customer lists, pricing proposals, and trade secrets.

1.3. **Exclusions:** Confidential Information shall not include any information that:
(a) is or becomes publicly available without breach of this Agreement by the Receiving Party;
(b) was already known to the Receiving Party prior to disclosure without any obligation of confidentiality, as evidenced by written records;
(c) is independently developed by the Receiving Party without reference to or reliance upon the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information; or
(d) is rightfully received from a third party without restriction and without breach of any confidentiality obligation.

---

### SECTION 2: OBLIGATIONS OF THE RECEIVING PARTY

2.1. **Standard of Care:** The Receiving Party agrees to protect the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information with the same degree of care it uses to protect its own confidential information of like kind, but in no event less than a reasonable degree of care.

2.2. **Permitted Use:** The Receiving Party shall use the Confidential Information solely for the Permitted Purpose and for no other commercial or personal purpose.

2.3. **Restricted Disclosure:** The Receiving Party shall not disclose Confidential Information to any third party except to its employees, officers, legal counsel, and financial advisors (collectively, "**Representatives**") who have a strict "need to know" such information for the Permitted Purpose and who are bound by confidentiality obligations at least as restrictive as those contained herein. The Receiving Party shall remain fully responsible for any breach of this Agreement by its Representatives.

---

### SECTION 3: TERM AND DURATION OF CONFIDENTIALITY

3.1. **Term of Agreement:** This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and shall continue in full force for a period of two (2) years, unless terminated earlier in accordance with Section 3.2.

3.2. **Termination for Convenience:** Either Party may terminate this Agreement at any time upon thirty (30) days' prior written notice to the other Party.

3.3. **Duration of Confidentiality Obligations:** The Receiving Party's obligations under Section 2 with respect to Confidential Information disclosed during the Term shall survive the termination or expiration of this Agreement for a period of five (5) years from the date of initial disclosure; provided, however, that with respect to any Confidential Information that constitutes a trade secret under applicable law (including the Defend Trade Secrets Act), such obligations shall survive in perpetuity for as long as such information remains a trade secret.

---

### SECTION 4: RETURN OR DESTRUCTION OF CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION

4.1. Upon written request of the Disclosing Party or upon termination of this Agreement, the Receiving Party shall promptly, and in any event within fourteen (14) calendar days, return or destroy all tangible materials containing or embodying the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information. An authorized executive officer of the Receiving Party shall certify such destruction in writing upon request.

4.2. Notwithstanding Section 4.1, the Receiving Party may retain one archival copy of Confidential Information solely in its secure legal archives for compliance, regulatory, or litigation defense purposes, subject to ongoing confidentiality obligations.

---

### SECTION 5: INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY AND NO LICENSE

5.1. **Ownership:** All Confidential Information remains the exclusive property of the Disclosing Party. Nothing in this Agreement grants either Party any rights, title, license, patent, copyright, trademark, or other intellectual property rights in or to the other Party's information, except the limited right to evaluate the Permitted Purpose.

5.2. **No Work-for-Hire or Assignment:** Neither Party assigns, transfers, or conveys any intellectual property rights under this Agreement. No joint work or work-for-hire is created or contemplated hereunder.

---

### SECTION 6: LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

6.1. **Direct Damages Cap:** EXCEPT FOR CLAIMS ARISING OUT OF A BREACH OF SECTION 2 (CONFIDENTIALITY) OR SECTION 8 (NON-SOLICITATION), NEITHER PARTY'S AGGREGATE CUMULATIVE LIABILITY UNDER OR RELATING TO THIS AGREEMENT SHALL EXCEED FIFTY THOUSAND U.S. DOLLARS ($50,000 USD).

6.2. **Consequential Damages Exclusion:** In no event shall either Party be liable for indirect, incidental, special, exemplary, or punitive damages, even if advised of the possibility of such damages.

---

### SECTION 7: INDEMNIFICATION

7.1. **Mutual Indemnification for Confidentiality Breach:** Each Party agrees to defend, indemnify, and hold harmless the other Party, its officers, directors, and employees from and against any third-party claims, liabilities, losses, and reasonable legal costs resulting directly from any unauthorized disclosure or intentional misappropriation of Confidential Information by the indemnifying Party or its Representatives.

7.2. Liability arising under Section 7.1 is not subject to the monetary cap set forth in Section 6.1.

---

### SECTION 8: NON-SOLICITATION OF EMPLOYEES

8.1. During the term of this Agreement and for a period of twelve (12) months following its termination or expiration, neither Party shall directly or indirectly solicit, recruit, or hire any technical or executive employee of the other Party with whom it had direct contact during the evaluation of the Permitted Purpose; provided that general public job advertisements not specifically targeted at such employees shall not constitute a violation of this provision.

---

### SECTION 9: AUDIT AND COMPLIANCE VERIFICATION

9.1. **Audit Rights:** Upon thirty (30) days' advance written notice, the Disclosing Party may designate an independent certified third-party auditor to inspect the Receiving Party's relevant electronic access logs and security records solely to confirm compliance with the data handling and destruction requirements of Section 4. Such audit shall occur no more than once per twelve-month period during normal business hours and shall not unreasonably disrupt the Receiving Party's operations.

---

### SECTION 10: DISPUTE RESOLUTION AND GOVERNING LAW

10.1. **Governing Law:** This Agreement, and any disputes arising out of or related to it, shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the substantive laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict of law principles.

10.2. **Dispute Resolution:** In the event of any controversy or claim arising out of or relating to this Agreement, the Parties shall first attempt in good faith to resolve the dispute through executive-level negotiations within twenty (20) business days. If unresolved, the dispute shall be finally settled by confidential binding arbitration administered by the American Arbitration Association ("**AAA**") in accordance with its Commercial Arbitration Rules, before a single arbitrator seated in Wilmington, Delaware.

---

### SECTION 11: FORCE MAJEURE

11.1. Neither Party shall be liable for any failure or delay in performing its obligations under this Agreement (other than obligations of confidentiality and non-disclosure) resulting from acts of God, flood, fire, war, terrorism, civil disturbance, pandemics, strikes, or government action beyond its reasonable control; provided that the affected Party provides prompt written notice and resumes performance as soon as practicable.

---

### SECTION 12: ASSIGNMENT

12.1. Neither Party may assign or transfer this Agreement, in whole or in part, without the prior written consent of the other Party, except that either Party may assign this Agreement without consent to an affiliate or in connection with a merger, acquisition, corporate reorganization, or sale of substantially all of its assets.

---

### SECTION 13: DATA PRIVACY AND SECURITY

13.1. To the extent any Confidential Information contains personal data subject to applicable data protection laws (such as GDPR or CCPA), the Receiving Party shall implement appropriate administrative, physical, and technical safeguards to protect such personal data against unauthorized processing or disclosure, and shall notify the Disclosing Party in writing within forty-eight (48) hours of becoming aware of any confirmed security breach impacting such data.

---

### SECTION 14: EXPORT COMPLIANCE

14.1. The Parties acknowledge that Confidential Information may be subject to U.S. export administration laws and regulations (EAR) and OFAC sanctions. Each Party agrees not to export, re-export, or transfer any technical data received hereunder to any prohibited destination or entity without required governmental authorization.

---

### SECTION 15: MISCELLANEOUS AND ENTIRE AGREEMENT

15.1. **Severability:** If any provision of this Agreement is held to be invalid or unenforceable by an arbitrator or court of competent jurisdiction, such provision shall be enforced to the maximum extent permissible, and the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect.

15.2. **Entire Agreement:** This Agreement constitutes the complete and exclusive agreement between the Parties with respect to the subject matter hereof and supersedes all prior and contemporaneous discussions, proposals, and understandings. No amendment shall be effective unless in writing and signed by authorized representatives of both Parties.

15.3. **Absence of Commercial Terms:** The Parties acknowledge that this is a non-disclosure agreement. No payment terms, product warranties, insurance requirements, or subcontracting provisions are established hereunder; any such terms shall be governed exclusively by future definitive commercial agreements, if any.
