# ENTERPRISE MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

**Agreement Number:** MSA-2024-9902  
**Effective Date:** April 15, 2024  
**Dataset Version:** v1.0  

This Enterprise Master Services Agreement ("**MSA**" or "**Agreement**") is entered into by and between:

1. **Nexus Global Enterprise Solutions, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation with its principal executive offices at 1301 Avenue of the Americas, 35th Floor, New York, NY 10019 ("**Nexus**" or "**Provider**"); and
2. **Apex Financial Technologies Ltd.**, a Delaware corporation with its principal executive offices at 200 West Street, 18th Floor, New York, NY 10282 ("**Apex**" or "**Customer**").

Provider and Customer are collectively referred to as the "**Parties**" and individually as a "**Party**."

---

### RECITALS

WHEREAS, Customer operates regulated global financial transaction and wealth management software platforms; and

WHEREAS, Provider offers enterprise cloud infrastructure engineering, bespoke algorithmic software development, and 24/7 mission-critical managed cloud operations; and

WHEREAS, Customer desires to engage Provider to deliver such enterprise professional services, cloud infrastructure management, and custom software deliverables pursuant to Statements of Work ("**SOWs**") governed by this MSA;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual promises, covenants, and representations herein, the Parties agree as follows:

---

### SECTION 1: CONTRACT STRUCTURE AND ORDER OF PRECEDENCE

1.1. **Structure:** This Agreement sets forth the core legal terms governing all services provided by Provider to Customer. Specific projects and deliverables will be documented in SOWs executed pursuant to the framework set forth in **Schedule A**.

1.2. **Order of Precedence:** In the event of any direct conflict or inconsistency between the terms of this Agreement and any exhibit, schedule, or SOW, the conflict shall be resolved according to the following descending order of priority:
(a) The Data Protection and Security Addendum (**Schedule B**);
(b) The main body of this Agreement (Sections 1 through 18);
(c) The Service Level Agreement (**Schedule C**);
(d) The Statement of Work Framework (**Schedule A**); and
(e) An individual Statement of Work (unless an individual SOW expressly identifies a specific section of this MSA that it intends to amend or supersede).

---

### SECTION 2: PERFORMANCE OF SERVICES AND STATEMENTS OF WORK

2.1. **Standard of Performance:** Provider shall perform all services and develop all deliverables in a professional, workmanlike manner, in strict accordance with highest industry standards, and in compliance with all agreed functional specifications and milestones set forth in each SOW.

2.2. **Key Personnel:** Provider shall assign the key individuals named in each SOW and shall not reassign or replace such individuals without Customer's prior written consent, except in cases of illness, resignation, or termination of employment.

---

### SECTION 3: INVOICING, FEES, AND PAYMENT TERMS

3.1. **Fees:** Customer shall pay Provider the fees set forth in each SOW, calculated on a time-and-materials or fixed-fee milestone basis as designated therein.

3.2. **Payment Terms:** Undisputed invoices shall be due and payable within forty-five (45) calendar days from Customer's receipt of a complete, detailed electronic invoice ("**Net 45**").

3.3. **Late Payments:** Any undisputed amounts not paid within the Net 45 period shall accrue interest at a rate of one percent (1.0%) per month or the highest rate permitted by applicable law, whichever is lower.

3.4. **Invoice Disputes:** Customer may withhold payment of disputed invoice items by providing written notice within twenty (20) calendar days of receipt, stating the specific reason for dispute. Withholding disputed amounts shall not constitute a default.

---

### SECTION 4: DELIVERABLES, INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY, AND SUBCONTRACTING

4.1. **Pre-Existing IP:** Each Party retains all rights in its pre-existing intellectual property, patents, copyrights, proprietary methodologies, and trade secrets ("**Background IP**").

4.2. **Ownership of Deliverables (Work-for-Hire):** All custom software, custom source code, documentation, architectures, and work product developed specifically for Customer under this Agreement or any SOW ("**Deliverables**") shall constitute "works made for hire" under the United States Copyright Act. Customer shall own all right, title, and interest in and to all Deliverables upon payment. Provider assigns to Customer all rights, title, and interest in such Deliverables.

4.3. **License to Provider Background IP:** To the extent any Deliverables incorporate Provider Background IP, Provider hereby grants Customer a perpetual, irrevocable, worldwide, royalty-free, fully paid-up, non-exclusive license (with right to sublicense) to use, reproduce, modify, and display such Background IP solely as integrated into or necessary for the operation of the Deliverables.

4.4. **Customer Data:** All data, records, financial transactions, and confidential customer information processed by Provider remain the sole and exclusive property of Customer.

4.5. **Subcontracting Rights:** Provider shall NOT subcontract any part of the services or development work without Customer's prior express written authorization. In the event Customer approves a subcontractor, Provider shall:
(a) bind such subcontractor to written obligations at least as restrictive as those in this Agreement (specifically including Schedule B); and
(b) remain directly and primarily liable for the performance, acts, omissions, and breaches of all subcontractors.

---

### SECTION 5: WARRANTIES AND REMEDIES

5.1. **Deliverable Warranty Period:** Provider warrants that for a period of one hundred and eighty (180) calendar days following written acceptance of each Deliverable by Customer (the "**Warranty Period**"), the Deliverable will conform in all material respects to its specifications and will be free from material defects, malicious code, trojans, backdoors, and logic bombs.

5.2. **Warranty Remedies:** If Customer notifies Provider of a warranty non-conformance during the Warranty Period, Provider shall, at its sole cost and expense:
(a) promptly rectify, repair, or replace the non-conforming Deliverable within fifteen (15) calendar days; or
(b) if unable to rectify within such time, refund all fees paid for the defective Deliverable and any associated dependent services.

5.3. **Regulatory Warranty:** Provider warrants that its services and infrastructure comply with all applicable laws and regulations governing financial data processing, including GLBA, SOC 2, and PCI-DSS.

---

### SECTION 6: CONFIDENTIALITY AND NON-DISCLOSURE

6.1. **Confidentiality:** Each Party agrees to maintain the confidentiality of all non-public information disclosed by the other Party, using at least a reasonable standard of care.

6.2. **Duration of Obligations:** Confidentiality obligations under this Section 6 shall survive the termination or expiration of this Agreement for a period of five (5) years; provided, however, that with respect to:
(a) trade secrets;
(b) source code;
(c) financial transaction records; and
(d) Personally Identifiable Information (PII) or Non-Public Personal Information (NPI),
such obligations shall survive in perpetuity.

---

### SECTION 7: NON-SOLICITATION OF PERSONNEL

7.1. During the term of this Agreement and for a period of eighteen (18) months following the effective date of termination or expiration of this Agreement, neither Party shall directly or indirectly recruit, solicit, induce, or employ any personnel or contractor of the other Party who performed services under any SOW without the other Party's prior written consent.

7.2. **Liquidated Damages:** If either Party breaches Section 7.1, the breaching Party shall pay as reasonable liquidated damages (and not a penalty) an amount equal to fifty percent (50%) of the hired individual's annualized base salary.

---

### SECTION 8: AUDIT RIGHTS AND REGULATORY INSPECTIONS

8.1. **Customer Audit Rights:** Upon fifteen (15) business days' advance written notice, Customer, its internal audit staff, or an independent certified public accounting firm (e.g., Big Four accounting firm) appointed by Customer shall have the right to audit and inspect Provider's books, records, systems, facilities, policies, and operational controls relating to the services and Deliverables.

8.2. **Frequency and Scope:** Such audits may be conducted once per calendar year during normal business hours; provided that in the event of:
(a) a confirmed Security Incident;
(b) a regulatory inquiry from a banking or securities authority (e.g., SEC, FINRA, OCC); or
(c) a reasonable suspicion of material non-compliance,
Customer may conduct an immediate audit without limitation on frequency.

8.3. **Cost of Audit:** If an audit reveals a billing overcharge exceeding three percent (3%) or a material deficiency in security controls under Schedule B, Provider shall promptly reimburse Customer for the reasonable out-of-pocket costs of the audit.

---

### SECTION 9: INSURANCE REQUIREMENTS

9.1. Provider shall maintain at its sole cost, with insurers rated A- VIII or higher by A.M. Best, the following policies throughout the term of this Agreement and for three (3) years thereafter:
(a) **Commercial General Liability (CGL):** $5,000,000 per occurrence and aggregate;
(b) **Cyber Risk, Network Security, and Technology Errors & Omissions (E&O):** Minimum limit of $10,000,000 per claim and aggregate, with coverage for regulatory fines, data restoration, and notification costs;
(c) **Commercial Crime and Fidelity Bond:** Minimum limit of $2,000,000 per occurrence;
(d) **Workers' Compensation & Employer's Liability:** Statutory limits and $1,000,000 per accident.

9.2. Customer shall be named as an additional insured on all liability policies, and Provider shall provide thirty (30) days' notice of policy cancellation or material reduction.

---

### SECTION 10: TERM AND TERMINATION

10.1. **Term:** This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues for an initial period of three (3) years, unless terminated earlier pursuant to this Section 10.

10.2. **Termination for Convenience:** Customer may terminate this Agreement or any individual SOW for convenience, without penalty or fee, upon ninety (90) calendar days' prior written notice to Provider. Provider shall have NO right to terminate this Agreement or any SOW for convenience.

10.3. **Termination for Cause:** Either Party may terminate this Agreement immediately upon written notice if the other Party:
(a) materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure such breach within thirty (30) calendar days of written notice; or
(b) becomes insolvent, makes an assignment for the benefit of creditors, or files for bankruptcy.

10.4. **Immediate Termination for Security Breach:** Customer may terminate this Agreement immediately without cure period in the event Provider suffers a material breach of Schedule B or unauthorized disclosure of Customer Data.

---

### SECTION 11: LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

11.1. **Consequential Damages Waiver:** Except as provided in Section 11.4, neither Party shall be liable for special, indirect, incidental, or consequential damages.

11.2. **General Aggregate Liability Cap:** Except as expressly set forth in Sections 11.3 and 11.4, each Party's maximum aggregate cumulative liability arising out of or related to this Agreement under any legal theory shall be limited to the total fees paid or payable by Customer under this Agreement in the twelve (12) months immediately preceding the event giving rise to liability.

11.3. **Enhanced Super-Cap for Data Security and Confidentiality:** Notwithstanding Section 11.2, for any liability arising out of or related to:
(a) Provider's breach of **Schedule B** (Data Protection and Security);
(b) Breach of Section 6 (Confidentiality); or
(c) Unauthorized exposure, loss, or corruption of Customer Data,
Provider's aggregate cumulative liability shall be subject to an **Enhanced Super-Cap equal to five million U.S. Dollars ($5,000,000 USD)** or three times (3x) the fees paid under this Agreement, whichever is greater.

11.4. **Uncapped Liabilities:** The limitations and exclusions in Sections 11.1, 11.2, and 11.3 shall NOT apply to:
(a) Either Party's indemnification obligations under Section 12;
(b) Either Party's gross negligence, willful misconduct, or intentional fraud; or
(c) Breach of Section 4.2 (Customer Deliverable Ownership).

---

### SECTION 12: INDEMNIFICATION

12.1. **Provider Indemnification:** Provider shall defend, indemnify, and hold harmless Customer, its parent companies, subsidiaries, affiliates, directors, officers, employees, and agents from and against all third-party claims, lawsuits, administrative penalties, liabilities, losses, damages, and reasonable attorneys' fees arising out of:
(a) Any claim that the services or Deliverables infringe or misappropriate any patent, copyright, trademark, trade secret, or other intellectual property right of any third party;
(b) Provider's gross negligence, intentional misconduct, or violation of applicable regulatory laws; or
(c) Any bodily injury, death, or tangible property damage caused by Provider or its personnel.

12.2. **Indemnification is Uncapped:** In accordance with Section 11.4(a), all obligations and liabilities arising under this Section 12 are strictly uncapped and not subject to any monetary liability ceiling.

---

### SECTION 13: DATA PRIVACY, CYBERSECURITY, AND INCIDENT NOTIFICATION

13.1. Provider shall comply with all data privacy and cybersecurity standards set forth in **Schedule B**.

13.2. **Incident Notification Window:** Provider shall immediately notify Customer in writing, and in any event within **twenty-four (24) hours** of confirming or having reasonable grounds to suspect any security breach, ransomware event, unauthorized access, or accidental loss of Customer Data.

---

### SECTION 14: FORCE MAJEURE

14.1. Neither Party shall be liable for delay or failure to perform caused by events beyond its reasonable control, including acts of God, war, riot, fire, flood, hurricane, or government decrees.

14.2. **Exclusion of IT Failures:** Routine hardware failures, software bugs, carrier outages, or foreseeable cybersecurity incidents shall NOT be considered force majeure events unless resulting directly from catastrophic warfare.

14.3. **Termination Right:** If a force majeure event prevents either Party from fulfilling its material obligations for a period of thirty (30) consecutive calendar days, Customer may terminate this Agreement immediately upon written notice without penalty.

---

### SECTION 15: ASSIGNMENT AND CHANGE OF CONTROL

15.1. Neither Party may assign or transfer this Agreement, whether by operation of law or otherwise, without the prior written consent of the other Party; provided that Customer may assign this Agreement to an affiliate without consent.

15.2. In the event of a Change of Control of Provider to an entity that is a direct commercial competitor of Customer in the financial services or financial technology sectors, Customer shall have the right to terminate this Agreement immediately with thirty (30) days' notice.

---

### SECTION 16: DISPUTE RESOLUTION AND ARBITRATION

16.1. **Three-Tier Escalation:**
(a) **Tier 1 - Executive Negotiation:** Any dispute shall first be referred to executive vice presidents of each Party for good faith negotiations over a fifteen (15) day period.
(b) **Tier 2 - Mediation:** If unresolved, the Parties shall submit the dispute to non-binding mediation administered by the International Institute for Conflict Prevention & Resolution (CPR) in New York, NY for thirty (30) days.
(c) **Tier 3 - Binding Arbitration:** If mediation fails, the dispute shall be finally settled by confidential binding arbitration administered by the American Arbitration Association (AAA) under its Commercial Arbitration Rules, before a panel of three (3) neutral commercial arbitrators seated in New York, New York.

---

### SECTION 17: GOVERNING LAW AND VENUE

17.1. This Agreement, and all claims or causes of action arising out of or related to this Agreement, shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the substantive laws of the **State of New York**, without giving effect to any choice of law or conflict of law principles.

17.2. The state and federal courts located in **New York County, New York** shall have exclusive jurisdiction for injunctive relief in aid of arbitration.

---

### SECTION 18: SEVERABILITY, EXPORT CONTROL, AND ENTIRE AGREEMENT

18.1. **Severability:** If any provision of this Agreement is held to be invalid or unenforceable, such provision will be modified to the minimum extent necessary, and the remaining provisions shall remain in full force.

18.2. **Entire Agreement:** This Agreement, together with Schedules A, B, and C, and all executed SOWs, constitutes the entire and exclusive agreement between the Parties with respect to the subject matter hereof, superseding all prior oral or written agreements.

18.3. **Export Compliance:** Both Parties shall comply with all applicable United States export control and trade sanctions laws and regulations, including the Export Administration Regulations (EAR) and International Traffic in Arms Regulations (ITAR).

---

## SCHEDULE A: STATEMENT OF WORK (SOW) FRAMEWORK

1. **Framework:** Each SOW shall specify deliverables, project schedules, acceptance criteria, pricing, and assigned key personnel.
2. **Acceptance Testing:** Customer shall have thirty (30) calendar days to perform user acceptance testing (UAT) on each Deliverable. Deliverables shall conform strictly to functional specifications before formal written acceptance.

---

## SCHEDULE B: DATA PROTECTION, SECURITY, AND CYBER STANDARDS ADDENDUM

1. **Information Security Obligations:** Provider shall maintain an information security program meeting SOC 2 Type II, ISO/IEC 27001, and NIST CSF standards.
2. **Encryption:** All Customer Data must be encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256).
3. **Breach Notification:** Provider shall alert Customer's Chief Information Security Officer (CISO) in writing within twenty-four (24) hours of any suspected or confirmed unauthorized access or compromise of Customer Data.
4. **Enhanced Liability Super-Cap:** As referenced in Section 11.3, Provider's liability for breaches of this Schedule B is capped at the Enhanced Super-Cap of $5,000,000 USD or 3x fees, whichever is greater.
5. **Audits of Security:** Customer or its designated Big-4 audit representative may conduct on-site security inspections upon fifteen (15) business days' notice, or immediately upon a confirmed incident.

---

## SCHEDULE C: SERVICE LEVEL AGREEMENT (SLA) AND WARRANTIES

1. **System Availability:** 99.95% monthly uptime excluding pre-approved scheduled maintenance.
2. **Warranty Period:** One hundred and eighty (180) days warranty following final acceptance of each Deliverable, as set forth in Section 5.1 of the Agreement.
3. **Disaster Recovery:** Recovery Time Objective (RTO) of four (4) hours; Recovery Point Objective (RPO) of one (1) hour.
