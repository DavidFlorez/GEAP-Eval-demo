# CLOUD VENDOR SERVICES AGREEMENT

**Agreement Reference:** VSA-2024-4190  
**Effective Date:** March 1, 2024  
**Dataset Version:** v1.0  

This Cloud Vendor Services Agreement ("**Agreement**") is made and entered into by and between:

1. **DataStream Analytics Corp.**, a Delaware corporation having its principal operational headquarters at 425 Market Street, Suite 1800, San Francisco, California 94105 ("**Vendor**" or "**DataStream**"); and
2. **BlueRidge Retail Holdings, Inc.**, a California corporation having its principal place of business at 950 Tower Lane, Foster City, California 94404 ("**Customer**" or "**BlueRidge**").

Vendor and Customer may individually be referred to as a "**Party**" and collectively as the "**Parties**."

---

### RECITALS

WHEREAS, Vendor has developed and operates a proprietary real-time cloud data pipeline and predictive customer analytics software-as-a-service platform (the "**Subscription Service**"); and

WHEREAS, Customer desires to subscribe to and access the Subscription Service, and Vendor agrees to provide such services subject to the terms and conditions set forth herein;

NOW, THEREFORE, the Parties agree as follows:

---

### SECTION 1: PROVISION OF SUBSCRIPTION SERVICES

1.1. **Access Grant:** Subject to the terms of this Agreement, Vendor grants to Customer a non-exclusive, non-transferable, revocable right during the Subscription Term to access and utilize the Subscription Service solely for Customer's internal business analytics operations.

1.2. **Service Level Agreement (SLA):** Vendor shall use commercially reasonable efforts to make the Subscription Service available with an uptime percentage of at least ninety-nine and nine-tenths percent (99.9%) during each calendar month, excluding scheduled maintenance.

---

### SECTION 2: INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY RIGHTS AND OWNERSHIP

2.1. **Vendor Proprietary Rights:** Vendor retains all right, title, and interest (including all patent rights, copyrights, trade secrets, trademarks, and all other intellectual property rights) in and to the Subscription Service, underlying algorithms, APIs, documentation, software engines, and any enhancements, modifications, or derivative works thereof. Customer is granted only the limited access rights expressly set forth in Section 1.1.

2.2. **Customer Data Ownership:** As between the Parties, Customer owns all right, title, and interest in and to all electronic data, transactions, customer records, and files submitted by Customer into the Subscription Service ("**Customer Data**"). Customer grants Vendor a worldwide, royalty-free, limited license to host, copy, process, and transmit Customer Data solely to the extent necessary to provide the Subscription Service.

2.3. **Analytics Output:** Customer shall own all bespoke analytical reports, exported charts, and aggregated metric summaries generated specifically for Customer by its use of the Subscription Service ("**Work Product**"); provided that Vendor's underlying models and general database schemas remain Vendor IP.

---

### SECTION 3: FEES, INVOICING, AND PAYMENT TERMS

3.1. **Subscription Fees:** Customer shall pay all subscription fees specified in the applicable Order Form. Unless otherwise specified in an Order Form, all fees are quoted in U.S. Dollars ($ USD).

3.2. **Payment Terms:** All invoices are due and payable within thirty (30) days from the date of the electronic invoice ("**Net 30**").

3.3. **Late Payments:** Any unpaid amounts not subject to a good-faith dispute shall accrue late payment interest at the rate of one and one-half percent (1.5%) per month, or the maximum rate permitted by law, whichever is less, calculated daily from the due date until paid.

3.4. **Taxes:** Fees do not include any applicable sales, value-added, or withholding taxes, which shall be Customer's responsibility.

---

### SECTION 4: CONFIDENTIALITY

4.1. **Definition:** Each Party agrees that all business, technical, operational, and financial information disclosed by one Party ("**Disclosing Party**") to the other ("**Receiving Party**") marked or identifiable as confidential constitutes Confidential Information. Customer Data is the Confidential Information of Customer.

4.2. **Protection Standard:** The Receiving Party shall hold Confidential Information in confidence, using at least the degree of care it exercises for its own confidential materials of similar nature, but not less than reasonable care.

4.3. **Survival Period:** The confidentiality obligations set forth herein shall survive the termination or expiration of this Agreement for a period of three (3) years; provided that trade secrets and Customer Data shall be protected perpetually until no longer confidential under applicable law.

---

### SECTION 5: DATA PROTECTION AND SECURITY

5.1. **Information Security Safeguards:** Vendor shall maintain rigorous administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to ensure the security, integrity, and confidentiality of Customer Data, maintaining SOC 2 Type II certification and ISO/IEC 27001 compliance throughout the Subscription Term.

5.2. **Security Incident Notification:** In the event of any confirmed unauthorized access, acquisition, exposure, or breach of security affecting Customer Data stored or processed by Vendor, Vendor shall notify Customer in writing without undue delay and in any event within seventy-two (72) hours of confirming the security breach.

5.3. **Privacy Compliance:** Vendor shall process Customer Data in compliance with applicable data privacy regulations, including the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

---

### SECTION 6: AUDIT RIGHTS AND COMPLIANCE VERIFICATION

6.1. **SOC 2 Report Access:** Upon Customer's written request, no more than once per twelve-month period, Vendor shall deliver to Customer a copy of Vendor's most recent third-party SOC 2 Type II audit report, subject to Customer executing an appropriate non-disclosure agreement.

6.2. **Limitation on Physical Audits:** Because Vendor operates a multi-tenant cloud environment, direct on-site physical inspections of Vendor's hosting facilities or server rooms are strictly prohibited, except where mandated by a government regulatory authority with direct regulatory oversight over Customer.

---

### SECTION 7: WARRANTIES AND REMEDIES

7.1. **Performance Warranty:** Vendor warrants that for a period of ninety (90) days from initial service provisioning (the "**Warranty Period**"), the Subscription Service will perform materially in accordance with the user documentation.

7.2. **Exclusive Remedy:** Customer's sole and exclusive remedy, and Vendor's entire liability for breach of Section 7.1, shall be for Vendor to make commercially reasonable efforts to correct the verified non-conformity, or, if Vendor cannot do so within thirty (30) days of notice, Customer may terminate the affected Order Form and receive a pro-rata refund of any prepaid, unearned subscription fees.

7.3. **Disclaimer:** EXCEPT AS EXPRESSLY SET FORTH HEREIN, VENDOR DISCLAIMS ALL OTHER WARRANTIES, EXPRESS OR IMPLIED, INCLUDING MERCHANTABILITY AND FITNESS FOR A PARTICULAR PURPOSE.

---

### SECTION 8: INDEMNIFICATION

8.1. **Vendor IP Indemnification:** Vendor shall defend, indemnify, and hold harmless Customer, its affiliates, directors, and officers from and against any third-party claim, suit, or proceeding alleging that Customer's authorized use of the Subscription Service infringes any valid United States copyright, trade secret, or patent. Vendor's total aggregate liability under this Section 8.1 shall be subject to the monetary cap of two million U.S. Dollars ($2,000,000 USD).

8.2. **Customer Indemnification:** Customer shall defend, indemnify, and hold harmless Vendor against any third-party claims or damages resulting from Customer Data that infringes third-party intellectual property or privacy rights or violates applicable law.

8.3. **Indemnity Procedure:** The indemnified Party must give prompt written notice of the claim, grant the indemnifying Party sole control of the defense and settlement, and provide reasonable cooperation.

---

### SECTION 9: LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

9.1. **Consequential Damages Waiver:** NEITHER PARTY SHALL BE LIABLE FOR ANY INDIRECT, SPECIAL, INCIDENTAL, CONSEQUENTIAL, OR PUNITIVE DAMAGES, INCLUDING LOSS OF PROFITS, DATA LOSS, OR BUSINESS INTERRUPTION, EVEN IF ADVISED OF THE POSSIBILITY OF SUCH DAMAGES.

9.2. **Aggregate Liability Cap:** EXCEPT FOR (A) EITHER PARTY'S BREACH OF CONFIDENTIALITY UNDER SECTION 4, (B) CUSTOMER'S INDEMNIFICATION OBLIGATIONS UNDER SECTION 8.2, OR (C) VENDOR'S INDEMNIFICATION OBLIGATIONS UNDER SECTION 8.1 (WHICH IS GOVERNED BY THE $2,000,000 CAP THEREIN), EACH PARTY'S TOTAL CUMULATIVE LIABILITY ARISING OUT OF OR RELATED TO THIS AGREEMENT SHALL BE STRICTLY LIMITED TO THE TOTAL FEES PAID OR PAYABLE BY CUSTOMER UNDER THIS AGREEMENT IN THE SIX (6) MONTHS PRECEDING THE INCIDENT GIVING RISE TO LIABILITY.

---

### SECTION 10: TERM AND TERMINATION

10.1. **Term:** This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues for an initial term of two (2) years ("**Initial Term**"), renewing automatically for successive one-year periods unless either Party provides notice of non-renewal at least sixty (60) days prior to the expiration of the then-current term.

10.2. **Termination for Convenience:** Customer may terminate this Agreement or any Order Form for convenience at any time upon sixty (60) days' advance written notice to Vendor; provided that Customer shall not be entitled to any refund of prepaid subscription fees. Vendor may not terminate for convenience during the Initial Term.

10.3. **Termination for Cause:** Either Party may terminate this Agreement immediately upon written notice if the other Party materially breaches any provision of this Agreement and fails to cure such breach within thirty (30) calendar days after receipt of written notice detailing the breach.

---

### SECTION 11: INSURANCE REQUIREMENTS

11.1. Vendor shall, at its own expense, maintain during the Term the following insurance coverage with reputable carriers rated A- VII or better by A.M. Best:
(a) **Commercial General Liability (CGL):** $2,000,000 aggregate, $1,000,000 per occurrence;
(b) **Worker's Compensation & Employer's Liability:** Statutory limits and $1,000,000 per accident;
(c) **Cyber Risk and Technology Errors & Omissions (E&O):** Minimum coverage of $5,000,000 per claim and in the annual aggregate, covering data breaches, unauthorized access, and network security failures.

11.2. Upon request, Vendor shall furnish Customer with a certificate of insurance naming Customer as an additional insured on the Commercial General Liability policy.

---

### SECTION 12: NON-SOLICITATION

12.1. During the Term and for a period of twelve (12) months following the effective date of termination or expiration of this Agreement, neither Party shall knowingly solicit for employment or hire any employee or contractor of the other Party who was directly involved in the performance or evaluation of this Agreement; provided that general recruitment advertisements shall not violate this clause.

---

### SECTION 13: SUBCONTRACTING RIGHTS

13.1. Vendor may utilize third-party subcontractors, including Tier-1 cloud hosting providers (e.g., Amazon Web Services or Google Cloud Platform) and data analytics infrastructure vendors, to perform aspects of the Subscription Service; provided, however, that Vendor remains fully responsible and liable for the acts, omissions, and performance of all such subcontractors to the same extent as if performed directly by Vendor employees.

---

### SECTION 14: EXPORT COMPLIANCE

14.1. Each Party agrees to comply with all applicable U.S. and foreign export control laws and trade sanctions, including the U.S. Export Administration Regulations (EAR) and regulations administered by the U.S. Department of the Treasury Office of Foreign Assets Control (OFAC). Customer represents that it is not located in any embargoed jurisdiction.

---

### SECTION 15: FORCE MAJEURE

15.1. Neither Party shall be liable for any failure or delay in performance resulting from causes beyond its reasonable control, including acts of God, war, terrorism, civil unrest, labor disputes, cyber-attacks of national scale, utility failures, or government embargoes.

15.2. If a force majeure event prevents performance for a continuous period exceeding forty-five (45) calendar days, either Party may terminate this Agreement immediately upon written notice without penalty or liability.

---

### SECTION 16: DISPUTE RESOLUTION AND ARBITRATION

16.1. **Executive Escalation:** Before commencing any formal proceeding, the Parties shall attempt to resolve any dispute arising out of this Agreement by elevating the matter to vice-president level executives for a period of thirty (30) days.

16.2. **Binding Arbitration:** Any dispute not resolved through negotiation shall be settled by confidential binding arbitration administered by JAMS under its Comprehensive Arbitration Rules and Procedures. The arbitration shall take place in San Francisco, California, before a single neutral arbitrator with at least ten years of commercial technology experience.

---

### SECTION 17: GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION

17.1. This Agreement shall be governed by, construed, and interpreted in accordance with the laws of the State of California, without giving effect to any principles of conflicts of law. Subject to Section 16, the federal and state courts located in San Francisco County, California, shall have exclusive jurisdiction over any proceeding seeking injunctive relief.

---

### SECTION 18: SEVERABILITY AND ENTIRE AGREEMENT

18.1. **Severability:** If any provision of this Agreement is determined to be invalid, illegal, or unenforceable, the validity and enforceability of the remaining provisions shall not be affected or impaired.

18.2. **Entire Agreement:** This Agreement, together with any executed Order Forms, exhibits, and schedules, constitutes the entire understanding between the Parties concerning the subject matter hereof and supersedes all prior negotiations, proposals, purchase orders, or agreements. No modification shall be binding unless in writing and signed by authorized representatives of both Parties.
